Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026

This policy explains what data ClipAgency, the desktop tool operated by Br3cki_clips, processes and why.

Whose data

ClipAgency signs in only to accounts owned by Br3cki_clips. It is not offered to other users, so it does not collect personal data from the public.

Data from TikTok

With our authorization, ClipAgency uses the TikTok API to receive:

  • Basic profile information of our own account (display name, avatar, profile details)
  • Account statistics (follower and like counts)
  • The list of videos on our account and their statistics
  • Access tokens needed to upload videos to our account

Data from YouTube

ClipAgency uses the YouTube Data and Analytics APIs to upload our videos, update our channel, read channel statistics and read and reply to comments on our own videos. Public comment text and commenter display names are processed only to show them to us and to draft replies.

How it is stored

  • All data is stored locally on our own computer. There is no external server or cloud database.
  • Access tokens are kept in a local file and used only to call the official APIs.
  • We do not sell, share or rent data to anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as needed to run our channels. Disconnecting an account in ClipAgency deletes its stored tokens. Access can also be revoked at any time in the TikTok or Google account settings.

Creators

For creators who give us permission, we store their name, channel links, the scope of their permission and the date it was given, so that we can credit them and prove the permission.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, and complain to a data protection authority.
